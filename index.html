01 A little backstory

Good things can
come from leftovers.

Seasquish began with waste, curiosity, and a lot of experiments. The idea: create anatomically correct sea-creature squishies from recycled materials, and make something joyful along the way.

The first one is a prototype—not the final form, but the beginning of something bigger. Every first squish helps us learn, improve, and dream up what comes next.
